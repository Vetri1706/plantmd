# PlantMD Demo Video Script

**Target Duration:** 2:00 - 2:30 minutes
**Tone:** Professional, Innovative, Solution-Oriented

---

## 0:00 - 0:15 | Intro & The Problem
**Visual:**
*   Start with the **Landing Page**.
*   Briefly scroll down to show the features grid and "How it works".
*   Highlight the "Powered by Gemini 3.0" badge.

**Voiceover:**
"Farmers lose 40% of their crops to disease every year due to delayed diagnosis. Introducing PlantMD—an AI-powered pathologist in your pocket. Built with Google's latest Gemini Multimodal models, it identifies diseases instantly and connects you to local solutions."

---

## 0:15 - 0:45 | Feature 1: Image Diagnosis & Non-Plant Detection
**Visual:**
1.  Click **"Start Free Diagnosis"** to go to the Upload screen.
2.  **Action (Error Case):** Upload a photo of a random object (e.g., a laptop or chair).
3.  Show the Alert: *"We could not detect a valid plant..."*.
4.  **Action (Success Case):** Upload a clear photo of a **Tomato leaf with blight**.
5.  Select "Tomato" from the dropdown.
6.  Click **"Analyze Image"**.
7.  Show the loading animation steps ("Scanning leaf structure...").

**Voiceover:**
"PlantMD is smart. If I upload a random object, the AI validates the image and rejects it, ensuring accuracy. But when I upload a sick tomato leaf, Gemini Pro analyzes the visual symptoms, texture, and severity in seconds."

---

## 0:45 - 1:15 | Feature 2: Results & Treatment Locator
**Visual:**
1.  Show the **Results View**.
2.  Highlight the disease name: **"Late Blight"** (Common name, easy to understand).
3.  Scroll down to **Treatments**.
4.  Point out "Organic Solutions" and "Chemical Options".
5.  **Action:** Click the **"Find"** button next to *"Copper Fungicide"*.
6.  Transition automatically to the **Dashboard Map**.
7.  Show the map populating with pins. Hover over the search bar showing: *"Searching for: Copper Fungicide"*.

**Voiceover:**
"We get a detailed diagnosis with common names and confidence scores. But here's the game-changer: I need Copper Fungicide. I simply click 'Find', and PlantMD uses Maps Grounding to locate actual stores nearby that stock this specific product, saving me hours of searching."

---

## 1:15 - 1:40 | Feature 3: Real Expert Network
**Visual:**
1.  Click on the **"Experts"** tab in the navigation.
2.  Show the loading spinner briefly.
3.  Show the grid of **Real Expert Profiles**. Hover over the "Verified Location" badge.
4.  Click "View on Maps" for one of the experts to show it opens Google Maps.

**Voiceover:**
"Sometimes AI isn't enough. The Experts tab scans my real-time location to find university extension offices and certified agronomists nearby. This isn't dummy data—these are real professionals I can contact immediately."

---

## 1:40 - 2:00 | Feature 4: History & Accessibility
**Visual:**
1.  Click on **"History"**.
2.  Show the list of past scans.
3.  Click **"View Report"** on an older item.
4.  Show the report re-opening with the saved image/video.
5.  Click the **"Listen to Diagnosis"** button.
6.  Show the waveform/audio icon animating.

**Voiceover:**
"Every scan is saved locally. I can revisit past reports anytime. And for accessibility, Gemini's Text-to-Speech engine can read the full diagnosis and treatment plan aloud to me while I work in the field."

---

## 2:00 - 2:30 | Outro & Video Analysis
**Visual:**
1.  Go back to **Upload**.
2.  Upload a **short video clip** of a plant moving in the wind.
3.  Click **"Analyze Video"**.
4.  Cut to the result showing it handled video input.
5.  Fade to black with PlantMD Logo.

**Voiceover:**
"From video analysis to real-time inventory checks, PlantMD leverages the full power of Gemini to secure our food supply. Try the demo today."
